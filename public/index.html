Visual context & privacy

When enabled, short factual topics may be sent to Wikipedia to find up to two reference cards. The app does not send your whole conversation to Wikipedia. Images load from Wikimedia and include source and licence links. Turn Visual context off to disable automatic lookups. This is an encyclopedia lookup, not a general news or web search. Personal-query filtering is a best-effort heuristic.
